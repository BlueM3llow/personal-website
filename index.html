Hey y'all! :]

my name is mel/mellow (she/they; non-binary). i am a multimedia artist that primarily focuses on furry illustrations and music composition (EDM and VGMs), but i also tend to dabble with 2D animation, video editing, modeling, and coding.

i've decided to create my little corner of the internet due to the current circumstances of social media, and its consistency to be unbearable to use. sometimes you just need to retreat back into a safe house.... this is my little safe house :] . that being said, my main goal with this website is to create a little place to archive my personal work, ramble about random shit, and have a place to share all of my brain with y'all with little to no friction.

at this current moment, this website is a MASSIVE wip... i will be adding onto the updates page whenever somethings has changed. check that out!

Wuts coming up?

i plan to add a LOT to this website; personal projects, batch scripts, an art gallery that works very similarly to boorus, my entire music discography (this will instead send you to a nav page that leads you to my Soundcloud or Bandcamp... once I start supporting Neocities i will overhaul my website :] ), just to name a few! i truly want this place to be my own personal island where anyone can peep at my work without the Dread of endlessly scrolling... we've gotta stop doomscrolling y'all.

expect maaaany things to be implemented! but of course, i do have ADHD so also expect many months of inactivity. this website overhaul came into existance because i decided to visually plan my website through figma. i also accepted that with the current knowledge i have with coding, it'd be best if i just keep things very very simple for now and build it all up. coding hard...
